# Security, privacy and implementation boundaries — version 2

## Enforced safeguards

Salted PBKDF2-SHA256 passwords; random hashed sessions; expiry, HttpOnly/SameSite cookies; validated same-origin JSON writes; login throttling; exact static route allowlist and restrictive CSP. Backend scopes, field allowlists, transactional conversions/approvals and foreign keys protect records independently of visible navigation.

Private HR and business files are stored outside the frontend directory under randomized names and require authorized download. Uploads are limited to 5 MB and a restricted extension set; known file signatures are checked for supported HR image/document types and business PDFs. No password/token/key/upload-body content is written into audit records.

Leader-only KPI ownership, 20%-default bidirectional change warnings, justification/confirmation and audit are enforced server-side. Employees cannot edit development plans. Course completion is not treated as a skill assessment. Time/difficulty/quality/revisions/context remain evidence rather than an automatic final performance or discipline score.

Anonymous feedback stores no employee ID. Employee screens use private browser bookmarks rather than ticket/reference numbers. Exact submission timestamps and ordinary access logs are deliberately avoided. HR must explicitly share a case before an owner can view/respond; anonymous identity remains absent. Raw narratives and individual survey responses are excluded from AI evidence. Surveys have monthly participation uniqueness and minimum-group aggregate suppression.

Owner access is company oversight rather than employee self-service. Salary, private personal documents and medical/complaint information do not become unrestricted owner data. Small-contributor salary-derived project costs are suppressed for ordinary commercial actors. Termination is a documented proposal/review/approval/offboarding process; AI has no mutation tool or authority to determine employment outcomes.

## Remaining limitations

1. **Live model acceptance is blocked by credentials.** Set `AI_API_KEY`, `AI_MODEL` and optionally `AI_BASE_URL`. The real transport and controlled success/error/privacy paths are tested; an actual model/account call cannot be verified without configuration. There is no demo/simulated answer fallback.
2. **CRM core is implemented, not enterprise feature parity.** There is no inbound mailbox/email synchronization, outbound email delivery, phone dialer, external calendar/advertising-platform synchronization, marketing automation builder, commercial document e-signature, tax invoice engine or external bank payment execution. Calls/emails/meetings are recorded activities. Reminders are in-app.
3. **Finance is operational intelligence, not accounting compliance.** Statutory payroll taxes, social insurance, local tax returns, bank files, revenue-recognition schedules and final-settlement accounting are not implemented. HR/accounting must review actual legal/contractual obligations. Client ad spend is separate from company fees. No arbitrary shared-contract or mixed-service revenue allocation is invented. Where cost/allocation evidence is missing, profit is unavailable.
4. **No unsupported Egyptian legal rules are hardcoded.** Individual contractual/company notice policies remain configurable. Proposed terminations require a documented HR/legal assessment and explicit authorization. This software does not establish legal grounds or calculate legal entitlements.
5. **Local development hosting only.** Python's built-in HTTP server is not a production application server. Production requires TLS/reverse proxy, process supervision, durable backup/recovery, deployment secrets and operational access review. No Docker image or hosted deployment is claimed as verified.
6. **Practical privacy, not formal anonymity.** A host/database administrator, timing observation, tiny cohorts, infrastructure logs or identifying narrative content can permit inference. Browser bookmarks are stored on the device; clearing browser storage loses anonymous follow-up access. Keep devices private and avoid identifying anonymous text. Aggregate suppression is not differential privacy.
7. **No enterprise identity or multi-company tenancy.** No SSO/MFA, password reset email, invitation delivery, external identity provider or fine-grained permission editor. Organization/users/roles/team supervision are configurable; role policy is implemented in code. Database and private files are not encrypted at rest. Upload malware scanning is not included.
8. **Attendance/location limits.** Browser location can be spoofed; no biometrics/device integration. Missing attendance is only a review indicator and never an automatic deduction. Host local time must match company time; the timezone setting is a descriptive label rather than a full timezone conversion engine.
9. **Leave policy depth.** Annual entitlement/counting/types/approval chains are configurable; accrual schedules, carryover, holiday calendars, hour-level non-overlap and post-approval cancellation/reversal are not implemented. Split cross-year requests. Approved payroll remains an immutable snapshot.
10. **Media and documents.** PDF CV extraction requires text-based PDFs; no OCR or credential verification. Large media should use a private asset reference; uploaded attachments are limited to 5 MB. There is no media transcoding/versioned binary asset repository. Deliverable content/review revisions are recorded in the application.
11. **Descriptive analytics.** Forecasts use recorded probabilities, actual stages and historical outcomes; snapshots permit comparison, not a predictive-model accuracy guarantee. Workload compares recorded backlog to a configured weekly reference; due dates, dependencies, uncertainty and part-time capacity require human interpretation. Training comparisons do not prove causation. Company salary-derived effort estimates use recorded current salaries rather than a historical labor-cost ledger, and small contributor groups are suppressed.
12. **UI/content language.** Navigation, forms and key states support Arabic/RTL. Submitted records, legacy report titles, course names, account names and configurable workflow question text retain their stored language. There is no automatic content translation. PDF Arabic shaping/font embedding is supported; a suitable installed font is required via `PDF_FONT_PATH` when platform defaults are absent.
13. **Reports cover core documents, not a report-builder suite.** Server PDFs cover monthly reports, payslips, quotes/proposals, projects/contracts and company/sales/client/department/performance summaries. There is no arbitrary visual report designer, scheduled emailed PDF distribution or financial allocation engine.
14. **Historical data remains historical.** Old generic KPI/report rows and prior fictional HR data are preserved, not silently rewritten into fabricated business evidence. New reports use profession-specific fields. New commercial records are entered by users/imports; QA fixtures are isolated from normal runtime data.

These are explicit boundaries rather than claims of enterprise, statutory, privacy or provider completeness. See `ACCEPTANCE.md` for evidence and the remaining live-provider check.
